# Deformed barrier and daughter channels

The static, axially symmetric shape is `R(theta)=R0 c[1+beta2 Y20(theta)+beta4 Y40(theta)]`, with real spherical harmonics and `c` chosen so its volume is `4 pi R0^3/3`. Both nuclear and charge radii use this shape. The implemented domain is `|beta2|, |beta4| <= 0.5`, with positive radius everywhere. The charge is uniform within that volume. Its Coulomb interaction with a point alpha is evaluated using even multipoles and analytic source-radius integrals, including the overlap region; the refined radial multipoles are tabulated at 0.01 fm. At zero deformation the spherical result is called directly.

The adiabatic approximation holds the orientation fixed during tunneling. Gauss-Legendre integration averages **penetrability**, not action or half-life, over isotropic orientations. The result reports the difference in log10 penetrability between successive angular orders and between charge calculations with multipole/source/radial resolutions (24, 256, 0.02 fm) and (32, 512, 0.01 fm). Either difference of 0.0001 or more produces a numerical error. A sampled Coulomb difference in MeV is also reported. These refinement comparisons are empirical diagnostics, not rigorous global error bounds. Assault frequency and preformation are orientation-independent inputs. This is a barrier model, not a coupled-channel calculation.

A channel has `Q = Qground - Ex`. Nonpositive Q is closed. With known parent and daughter spin/parity, integer orbital angular momenta must obey `|Jp-Jd| <= l <= Jp+Jd` and `parity_parent = parity_daughter * (-1)^l`; absent a specified l the lowest allowed l is chosen. Unknown spin requires an explicit l and cannot validate the selection rule. The implementation uses twice-spin integers. Each partial rate is `S_alpha * nu * P`; rates are summed in log space. Branches are conditional on alpha decay unless an independent competing non-alpha rate is supplied. Measured branching fractions are not model weights. Zero preformation has zero rate and an infinite partial half-life (represented by an absent logarithm).

References: [Denisov and Khudenko, Atomic Data and Nuclear Data Tables 95 (2009)](https://kinr.kyiv.ua/data/departments/NStrD/Denisov/Denisov-Khudenko-ADNDT95-815.pdf) discusses deformed radii, barriers, and alpha-decay calculations; [deformed two-potential calculation, Chinese Physics C](https://cpc.ihep.ac.cn/article/doi/10.1088/1674-1137/ae0430) describes angle-dependent WKB penetrability. These sources motivate the approximation; the uniform-charge overlap prescription and numerical thresholds here are implementation choices.


If a channel is unresolved or its barrier calculation fails with nonzero preformation, branching fractions are absent for the entire set because the denominator is incomplete. A validated zero-preformation channel has exactly zero rate even when its barrier diagnostic fails; its `barrier_status` remains available separately. Malformed rate inputs and unresolved selection rules are still rejected or marked unavailable. With no alpha rate, conditional alpha branches are undefined; an explicitly supplied positive competing rate still permits zero overall branches for closed or zero-rate channels.

## Native JSON and viewer

Use `--run` with the usual schema-1 fields and `potential_model` set to `square_well` or `woods_saxon`. Add `deformation: {"beta2": 0.2, "beta4": 0.05}` and/or a nonempty `channels` array. A channel accepts `name`, `excitation_mev`, optional `daughter_spin: {"twice_j": 4, "parity": 1}`, optional `l`, and optional per-channel `preformation` and `assault_hz`; omitted rate inputs inherit the request values. The optional `parent_spin` has the same `twice_j` and `parity` fields. If deformation is given without a channel list, the request's `l` supplies one ground-state channel. `competing_rate_hz` is an independent non-alpha rate, not a measured branch weight.

The response contains per-channel status, Q, chosen l, log10 penetrability, log10 partial rate and half-life, conditional alpha branch, and (when a competing rate is supplied) overall branch. Open channels also report angular and charge log10 refinement differences, angular point count, multipole order, and a sampled Coulomb difference. The aggregate fields `log10_total_alpha_rate_hz`, `log10_total_rate_hz`, and `log10_total_half_life_s` are null when the rate is zero or the channel set is incomplete. Without a competing rate the total is alpha-only; with one it includes that supplied rate.

In the viewer select a nuclear potential and enable **Deformation and daughter channels**. The channel editor supports adding and removing daughter states, specifying or deriving l, and per-channel rate inputs. Results and refinement diagnostics appear under **Channel results** after Calculate. The blue 3D surface follows the normalized nuclear radius; the gold spherical-reference turning surface and barrier depiction are schematic. Preview a nonzero shape with `AlphaDecay --render-preview image.png Po-212 1280 800 woods_saxon 0.2 0.05`.
